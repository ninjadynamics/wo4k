Roads use the full-colour PS1 tile libraries already present byte-for-byte in the PC WADs. They therefore count as PC reuse, not disc-required PS1 choices.

EXPLODE.CMP and SMOKE.CMP also remain PC reuse because the complete archives are byte-identical to PS1.

A PS1 source choice does not imply a resolution or colour improvement: different native headers/palettes/flags also prevent byte-identical reuse.
